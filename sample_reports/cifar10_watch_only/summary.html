What happened

The monitor checked the model every 50 steps over 5,083 training steps (3 h 16 min). At step 400 it found stuck channels in 2 layers, conv2.0 (Conv2d) and conv3.0 (Conv2d); 192 channels were proven dead, meaning they could never learn again on their own. They were still stuck when the run ended. At the last check (step 5,050), 248 channels in layer classifier.0 (Linear) were silent: no positive output for several checks in a row. They are likely unused, not proven dead, and are not counted as stuck. The training loss (the error the model is trained to reduce) jumped once, first at step 400. The learning signal of 8 layers fell to zero or moved far outside its own normal range (9 warnings, first at step 450).

What this report does not tell you

  • It does not make anyone compliant with the EU AI Act or any other rule. The record is evidence for technical documentation, not a certificate.
  • It does not measure how accurate or fit for purpose the model is. A run with no problems found can still produce a poor model.
  • It never reads the training data, so it cannot find mislabelled or harmful examples.
  • Proofs that a part of the model is dead apply only to one common building block (convolution or linear layer, then batch normalisation, then ReLU). Transformers and large language models get warnings only, never proofs.
  • The tool has so far been tested on one Apple-silicon Mac, not yet on NVIDIA/Linux, multiple GPUs or at large scale.

Words used here

Layer
One stage of the neural network. Layers have internal names such as conv2.0.
Channel
One small unit inside a layer. A layer has tens to thousands of them.
Stuck
A channel that, judging from its weights, outputs nothing useful: proven dead, a constant, or shrunk to almost zero.
Proven dead
A mathematical certificate that a channel can never learn again on its own, however long training continues.
Silent
A channel that gave no positive output for several checks in a row. Often normal; it is not proof that the channel is dead, and it is not counted as stuck.
Warning (alarm)
A layer's learning signal moved far outside that layer's own normal range.
Tamper-evident record
The log of everything the monitor saw. Each line carries a fingerprint of the line before it, so edits show.
